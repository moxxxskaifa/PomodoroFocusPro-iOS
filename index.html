Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

TL;DR: PomodoroFocusPro is 100% offline. We collect ZERO data. Everything stays on your device.

1. No Data Collection

PomodoroFocusPro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All your data (focus sessions, statistics, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

2. No Internet Required

This app works completely offline. It does not require an internet connection to function and does not make any network requests.

3. No Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your privacy is completely protected.

4. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally in your device's secure storage. You can export or delete your data at any time from the app settings.
